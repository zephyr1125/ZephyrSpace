## 7. SANOFI COLLABORATION ECONOMICS

### 7.1 Scope and mechanics `[实际/已发布]` (Q2 2026 10-Q Note 3a, verbatim where quoted)

**Scope:** *"a global collaboration with Sanofi to research, develop, and commercialize fully human monoclonal antibodies, which currently consists of **Dupixent (dupilumab), Kevzara (sarilumab), and itepekimab**."* (Note: itepekimab development has been **discontinued** — see §2.1 — but remains named in the agreement.)

**Development cost sharing:** *"Agreed-upon development expenses incurred by the Company and Sanofi are generally shared equally."*

**Commercialisation roles:** *"Sanofi leads commercialization activities for products under the collaboration, subject to the Company's right to co-commercialize such products. The Company co-commercializes Dupixent in the United States and in certain countries outside the United States. The Company also supplies certain commercial bulk product to Sanofi."*

**Profit split — the core economics:**
- **United States: profits are shared EQUALLY (50/50).**
- **Outside the United States: a SLIDING SCALE based on sales — starting at 65% Sanofi / 35% Regeneron, ending at 55% Sanofi / 45% Regeneron.**
- **Sanofi records the product sales** for commercialised products (i.e., Dupixent and Kevzara global net sales appear in Sanofi's P&L, not Regeneron's). **Regeneron books its share of profits as "Sanofi collaboration revenue."**

1. **The US EYLEA franchise is in structural decline but the HD transition is working:** total US EYLEA HD+EYLEA fell from $1,147.5M (Q2 2025) to $1,008.5M (Q2 2026), −12%; but EYLEA HD US rose +52% to $596.3M and **overtook legacy EYLEA for the first time**, reaching ~60% of US franchise sales vs 34% a year earlier. Legacy EYLEA US fell −45% to $412.2M.
2. **Only two aflibercept biosimilars are actually on the US market today (Pavblu, Yesafili), but Q4 2026 brings a launch cluster** (Enzeevu, Ahzantive, Eydenzelt on 2026-12-31, AVT06), and Samsung Bioepis Opuviz follows in **January 2027**.
